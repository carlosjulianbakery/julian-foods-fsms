Procedure for Scales: Place the scale on a flat surface, away from any air flow or vibration for best results. Place the 500 gram weight over the points seen in the image in each corner and center. The margin is 1.10 lb ± 0.1 lb. Report any irregularity, e.g.: broken parts, sanitation, low battery, unusual noises, among others.

Procedure for Portable Thermometer: Place a container with water and ice in equal parts, place the thermometer 1 cm deep in the ice water, for 2 minutes until reaching 32°F (0°C), Report any irregularities, e.g. parts broken or missing, sanitation, low battery.

Procedure for Non-Movable Thermometer: Use a portable thermometer that has been recently verified for accuracy, and use its reading as the target value. Compare the reading of the non-movable thermometer to the target reading. Report any irregularities.
